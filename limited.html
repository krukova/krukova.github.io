Task 1: Find the Limited-Edition Cards and Stickers

3xl - 40px

"Try to find limited-edition products with designs. Act the way you normally would when looking for a new product or service."

What we observe:

l - 16px

Does the participant go to a search engine ("limited cards", "card design", "bank card", etc.)

Does the participant navigate to the Bank website manually

Does the participant search via mobile suggestions, hints, or ad blocks

Does the participant land on the card catalogue or promo page on their own

Does the participant notice the "Bank-Card with Engraving" section in the menu or catalogue

Does the participant use the on-site search
